OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Demonstrate that in eventual consistency mode, a read immediately after
a write to a different replica may return stale or missing data.

2. Immediately read x from Replica 2
3. Wait for replication to complete
4. Read x from Replica 2 again
5. Check all replicas
